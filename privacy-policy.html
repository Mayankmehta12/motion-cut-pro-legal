Motion Cut Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Motion Cut Pro ("Motion Cut Pro", "we", "us", or "our") is a video editing application designed to help users create, edit, process, and export video content. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the app, why it is processed, how it is protected, and the choices available to you.

By using Motion Cut Pro, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where applicable, we will request the permissions or consent required for a particular feature.

2. Information We Process

The information processed by Motion Cut Pro depends on the features you use. We aim to process only information reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the application.

3. Account Information

If you create or use an account, we may process information associated with your authentication account, such as:

  • Email address associated with your account.
  • Display name, if you provide one.
  • Profile photo, if provided through a supported sign-in provider.
  • Authentication provider information.
  • A unique account identifier generated by the authentication service.
  • Anonymous account information when you use Motion Cut Pro as a guest.

4. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, authentication is handled through Google and our authentication infrastructure. Motion Cut Pro receives only the account information made available through the authentication flow and uses it to authenticate and maintain your Motion Cut Pro account.

Google may process information according to its own privacy policies. You should review Google\\

&#x20; s independent processing of your data.

5. Guest Accounts

Motion Cut Pro may allow you to continue as a guest. A guest account can be associated with an anonymous authentication identifier so that your session and application data can remain associated with the same account while you use the application.

You may later be offered the ability to upgrade or link the guest account to a permanent authentication method.

6. Video, Photos and Other Media

Motion Cut Pro may request access to photos, videos, audio files, or other media when you explicitly use an editing or import feature.

  • Selected media may be accessed to display previews, thumbnails, timeline clips, and editing controls.
  • Media may be processed locally on your device for editing and export.
  • Exported videos may be saved to your device or supported media library when you request an export.

Motion Cut Pro does not require access to your entire media library merely because the application is installed. Access should be limited to the functionality you choose to use and the permissions provided by your operating system.

7. Projects and Editing Data

Your projects may contain information such as project names, media references, clip positions, trimming information, transformations, audio settings, text layers, filters, transitions, and export settings.

Where the current application stores project information locally, that information remains on your device unless a feature explicitly requires it to be transmitted to a server or third-party service.

8. Exported Content

When you export a project, Motion Cut Pro processes the selected media and editing instructions to produce the requested output.

Exported files may be temporarily created in application storage during processing and may subsequently be saved to your device gallery or media library when you choose to do so.

9. Microphone and Audio

If Motion Cut Pro provides voice, recording, or audio-related features, microphone access may be requested only when you activate a feature that requires it.

Audio may be processed locally or transmitted to a service only when required by the particular feature and disclosed to you before use.

10. AI Features

Some Motion Cut Pro features may use artificial intelligence to assist with editing, analysis, generation, transcription, or other creative workflows.

When an AI feature requires content to leave your device, the app will use the applicable service necessary to provide that feature. The specific information processed will depend on the AI feature you choose to use.

We do not represent that every current version of Motion Cut Pro provides every AI capability described in this section. This section applies when such functionality is made available.

11. Firebase and Service Providers

Motion Cut Pro may use third-party infrastructure and service providers to provide application functionality, including authentication, security, storage, analytics, notifications, or other services.

For the current account system, Firebase services may process authentication-related information necessary to create, authenticate, and manage your account.

Third-party service providers are permitted to process information only for the purposes necessary to provide their services to us or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

12. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Motion Cut Pro may use notification services to deliver alerts such as export completion, processing status, product updates, or other notifications you have enabled.

You can control notification permissions through the application and/or your device operating-system settings.

13. Analytics and Diagnostics

If analytics or diagnostic services are enabled in a particular version of Motion Cut Pro, they may process technical or usage information such as application events, crash information, device configuration, operating-system information, and performance data.

Such information is used to understand application performance, identify crashes and errors, improve features, and maintain security.

14. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Motion Cut Pro is not designed to intentionally collect information such as your contacts, text messages, call history, health information, or precise location unless a future feature specifically requires such information and the applicable disclosure and permission are provided.

15. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your Motion Cut Pro account.
  • Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions.
  • Provide video editing and project functionality.
  • Process exports and other requested operations.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Send notifications that you have enabled.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate security or abuse.
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems.
  • Improve application reliability and functionality.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

16. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to provide the functionality of Motion Cut Pro, such as authentication, infrastructure, analytics, notifications, payment processing, or AI functionality that you explicitly use.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, security, and integrity of Motion Cut Pro, its users, or others.

17. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should also protect your device, account credentials, and authentication methods.

18. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain legitimate business and security functions, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

Locally stored projects and media may remain on your device until you delete them or your operating system removes the relevant data.

19. Account Deletion

If you have an account, Motion Cut Pro provides an in-app account deletion option. When you request account deletion, we will delete or de-identify account information under our control, subject to information that we are required or permitted to retain by law.

Deleting your account may not automatically delete files that you have independently saved to your device, gallery, cloud storage, or another service. You are responsible for deleting those copies if you no longer want them.

20. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the features available in your version of Motion Cut Pro, you may be able to:

  • Access and update profile information.
  • Change notification preferences.
  • Choose whether to use a guest or permanent account.
  • Control operating-system permissions.
  • Delete your Motion Cut Pro account.
  • Contact us regarding privacy questions or requests.

21. Children

Motion Cut Pro is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that violates applicable requirements, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

22. International Processing

Depending on the services used by Motion Cut Pro, information may be processed in countries other than the country in which you live. Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards for such processing.

23. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Motion Cut Pro changes, when new features are introduced, or when legal or regulatory requirements change.

The updated version will include a revised "Last updated" date. For material changes, we may provide additional notice when appropriate.

24. Contact Us

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns regarding Motion Cut Pro, please contact the developer using the support contact published in the application and its official store listing.
